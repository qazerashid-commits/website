Our Story

Welcome to Emirates Grills

Located on Plot no. 11 Kisementi Road in Kampala, Uganda, Emirates Grills is a highly-rated restaurant (4.5/5 stars) specializing in authentic Arabic cuisine. Our passion for Middle Eastern flavors brings you the finest grilled meats, traditional dishes, and refreshing mocktails in a welcoming atmosphere.

Culinary Team Highlights

Meet Our Kitchen Masters

Emirates Grills is driven by a distinguished culinary team focused on authentic Gulf and Middle Eastern cuisine, from grilled classics to handcrafted pastries.

  • The Head Chef

    Yemeni Culinary Director

    The kitchen is led by a Yemeni head chef who is frequently credited by reviewers for ensuring the authenticity of signature Gulf and Middle Eastern dishes such as Lahm Mandi and Laham Hanith.

  • Specialty Chef

    Chef Maitha

    Chef Maitha is celebrated for her leadership in Emirates Grills’ bakery and pastry selection, adding refined desserts and fresh breads to every guest experience.

  • Staff Mentions

    Jackie & John

    Long-term team members Jackie and John are highly rated for their expert menu knowledge and polished table-side service, helping guests navigate the menu with confidence.
